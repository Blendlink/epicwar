📋 Battle Plan

📖 Commander's Guide

1 · Prospect. Tap the glinting ⛏️ rare-earth ore rocks (gold & silver streaks) and the 🛢️ bubbling oil pits near your Command Center — the game tells you what each one is.

2 · Industrialize. Raw deposits can't be harvested. Build an Ore Mine Head ON ore and a Gas Pump Station ON an oil field. Your 👷 workers construct every building by hand — put more workers on one site and it rises faster.

3 · Haul. Workers mine at finished stations; 🚚 haul trucks collect the loads and drive them home to the Command Center. Train both at ⚔️ Army. No trucks — no income!

4 · Build a city. Every structure pours concrete and paves a street to your CC. Add 🌷 gardens, 🌳 trees and ⛲ fountains to make it home — raiders can burn them, so build 🧱 walls, turrets and bunkers too.

5 · Fight. Train an army, plan up to 3 attack waves, and raid enemy bases in auto-battles. Pass 50% destruction to loot; destroy their Command Center and everything chain-detonates for 100% loot. Fail, and the defenders loot you. Watch for revenge raids!

6 · Survive. New commanders are shielded until defenses hit 75% — but attacking anyone drops your shield instantly. If your CC falls, an allied convoy helps you rebuild.

7 · Direct units. All unit orders share one centralized command pipeline (select → route input → process order). Left-click a friendly unit to select it; left-click the ground to redirect / control-move every selected unit to that point. Right-click a unit or valid target to assign an activity (harvest, haul, guard, attack, or the default job for that unit type). The HUD flashes brief feedback when an order lands so you can confirm the click registered.

8 · Input safety. Clicks on menus, sheets, pills, action bars, and other UI chrome are filtered out before unit routing — they never issue unintended move or activity orders. Only world picks on the 3D field reach the command processor.
